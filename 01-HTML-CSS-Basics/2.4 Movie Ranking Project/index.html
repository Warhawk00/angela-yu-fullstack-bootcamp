My Favorite Movies

Top 3 Movies I watched last year

La la Land

La La Land is a 2016 American romantic musical film written and directed by Damien Chazelle. The film stars Ryan Gosling and Emma Stone as a musician and an aspiring actress who meet and fall in love in Los Angeles.

Inglourious Basterds

Inglourious Basterds is a 2009 action thriller film directed by Quentin Tarantino. The film follows a group of Jewish fighters who take the law into their own hands to fight crime in their city.

Sam Manekshaw

Sam Manekshaw is a 2016 biographical war film directed by James Smith. The film depicts the life of General Sam Manekshaw, a prominent military leader in the Indian Army.
